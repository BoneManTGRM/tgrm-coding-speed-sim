Why does τ ≈ 0.6 give ~40–50% gain?

When τ is too low, few micro-repairs trigger → you mostly pay full cost. When τ is too high, you over-apply micro-repairs, reducing efficiency. The sweet spot (τ≈0.5–0.7) balances both.
